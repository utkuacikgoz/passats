Before you put your name on it

What is PassATS?

PassATS is an ATS resume checker. You upload a PDF or DOCX, it reads the file the way an applicant tracking system does, scores it out of 100, and names the exact lines to change. It costs $2.99 once, needs no account, and stores nothing.

What is an ATS score and why should I care?

It is a hard look at the text in your resume. A low score often means key terms are missing, proof is thin, or structure is unclear. It is not a hiring decision. It is a chance to tighten your case before you send it.

How is this different from free ATS checkers?

Most tools give you a number and leave you alone with it. PassATS points to the exact lines that need work. You get the score, the reason, and the next move. $2.99. No account. No recurring charge.

Why am I applying to jobs and hearing nothing back?

Silence has many causes. Your resume may not show the terms, results, or level the role calls for. Start with the job description. Then make every bullet prove something real.

What file formats work?

PDF and DOCX up to 5 MB work. Use a PDF with selectable text. Avoid scanned files and image only documents.

What happens to my resume after I upload it?

We process it, analyze it, and discard it. We do not store it, train on it, or sell it. Stripe handles payment. We do not see your card details.

How do I make my resume ATS ready?

Use clear section headers. Use selectable text. Put skills and results in plain language. Match job terms where they are true. Use both acronyms and full names when they matter. Show outcomes, not duties.

What ATS score is good enough to pass?

Aim for 80 or higher. Treat the score as a guide, not a verdict. Fix the weakest area first. Then send a resume that does your work justice.
